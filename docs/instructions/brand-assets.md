# Messenger brand marks

Used as decorative brand identifiers beside the labelled contact links. The SVGs
are served locally and contain no scripts or external resources.

- Telegram and WhatsApp: path geometry from Simple Icons (CC0-1.0), with brand colours.
  https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
- MAX: brand SVG distributed in WP Ru-max, an integration with the Russian MAX messenger.
  Source: https://github.com/RuCoder-sudo/wp-ru-max/blob/06397e6c3c9ec844b77c310f82a17c9ac9a26a1c/assets/pro/MAX.svg
  Source distribution: GPL-2.0-or-later; a copy is in LICENSE-MAX-GPL-2.0-or-later.txt.
  SVG preserved with its own gradients; image-document isolation prevents duplicated page IDs.

Brand marks identify their respective services; they do not indicate affiliation.
